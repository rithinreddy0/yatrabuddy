# YatraBuddy data sources

- [Demo seed](backend/goa-escape.json) contains project-authored simulated fares, times, bookings, expenses and itineraries. Delay inputs come from the user. No kickoff document is used as a runtime dataset.
- [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs) supplies timestamped forecast and model conditions. Verify current service terms and usage limits before public deployment.
- [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) supplies maps with contributor attribution and ODbL terms. [OSRM](https://project-osrm.org/) supplies driving estimates. Verify the public routing server’s usage limits before deployment.
- [Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Palolem) and [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/) provide public destination references. The research views retain links to source articles.
- City briefings select short excerpts from verified Wikivoyage guide sections under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The [GOV.UK Content API](https://content-api.publishing.service.gov.uk/) verifies UK Foreign Office advisory links and keeps country guidance separate from named regional advice. The weather topic links to timestamped Open-Meteo model conditions.
- Place discovery uses OpenStreetMap through [Photon](https://github.com/komoot/photon/blob/master/docs/api-v1.md), Wikipedia coordinates and bounded Wikivoyage HTML extraction. The Wikivoyage crawler uses the official [HTML parsing endpoint](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Parsing_wikitext) when the REST HTML format is unavailable. It keeps canonical guide sections and listing URLs. Ordinary search queries use public sources first, and configured Google Places can answer verified review filters or fill empty public searches.
- Photo and video cards use individually licensed files from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Search_by_location). File pages, creator and license travel with the media. Photo thumbnails can fall back to the same licensed original when it is small enough. Video lookup includes Commons [browser playback variants](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Extension:TimedMediaHandler/API). Name-only clips may require checking the source for the location.
- Public providers have independent coverage and availability. Set `SEARCH_USER_AGENT` to the application name/version and a real operator email or contact URL according to the [Wikimedia User-Agent policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Wikimedia_Foundation_User-Agent_Policy). Wikimedia requests use short deadlines and respect [provider backoff](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Wikimedia_APIs/Rate_limits). Places can still be listed when a source has no licensed photo or the media provider is temporarily unavailable.
- Questions about sights, stays or dining in planning chat use the public place sources. Results link to their individual references and show when retrieved. They do not verify room availability, venue opening hours, or dietary accommodations.
- The saved-trip connection check uses the selected demo journey, the itinerary's planned total, and the [shared simulation rules](backend/connection-simulation.json). The delay, replacement prices, and connection outcomes are simulated.
- Travel Sources links directly to [IndiGo](https://www.goindigo.in/), [IRCTC](https://www.irctc.co.in/nget/train-search), [Uber](https://m.uber.com/), [redBus](https://www.redbus.in/), [Goa River Navigation](https://rnd.goa.gov.in/), [Goa Tourism](https://bookings.goa-tourism.com/Appln/UIL/ActivitiesHome), [Cordelia](https://www.cordeliacruises.com/), and [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/). Current quotes depend on dates, availability, and provider access. Booking.com in-app results require partner credentials.
- The desktop views reuse the existing bundled Goa image. Verify its provenance and reuse rights before distributing the submission.
- Landing cards and missing-photo illustrations reuse bundled travel imagery from `public/images`. Illustrative images do not identify individual venues. Confirm the existing bundled assets' distribution rights before public release.
- The booking lobby, calendar, rail transport, and travel journal images are generated decorative illustrations. They do not depict specific providers, bookings, or services.

## Conversational planning

Gemini supplies conversational prose and validated tool requests through a server-only API key. General advice is labelled as general knowledge, not live evidence. Public place, weather and image cards retain their source links. Wikimedia photos include the source credit and licence; missing photos remain unavailable. Model prose is delivered as a validated answer block, while tool progress is streamed.

Google account quotas apply. HTTP 429 responses are not retried immediately; the backend honors the provider retry interval and distinguishes a daily quota exhaustion from temporary rate limiting. Planning and saving remain available during an outage. No current commercial fare or booking availability is inferred from the model.
